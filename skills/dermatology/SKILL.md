# Dermatology

## Describe first (also when reading a photo)
Morphology (macule, papule, plaque, vesicle, bulla, pustule, nodule, ulcer), color, size in mm/cm, number, distribution (sun-exposed, flexural, dermatomal, acral, mucosal), configuration (annular, linear, grouped), surface (scale, crust), and evolution. Ask about onset, itch/pain, new drugs in the last 8 weeks, fever, mucosal involvement, travel, contacts, and immune status. A photo cannot show texture, warmth or blanching: ask.

## Emergencies (send to emergency care now)
- **Fever + non-blanching petechial/purpuric rash**: meningococcemia until proven otherwise.
- **SJS/TEN**: painful dusky skin, blisters or skin peeling, mucosal erosions (eyes, mouth, genitals), usually 4–28 days after a new drug (allopurinol, lamotrigine, carbamazepine, phenytoin, sulfonamides, NSAIDs). Stop the drug; burn/ICU care.
- **DRESS**: rash + fever + facial swelling + eosinophilia/organ involvement 2–8 weeks after a drug.
- **Necrotizing fasciitis**: pain out of proportion, rapid spread, crepitus, bullae, systemic toxicity.
- **Eczema herpeticum**: punched-out erosions on eczema, fever: urgent antivirals.
- Erythroderma (>90% of skin), angioedema with airway symptoms, toxic shock.

## Pigmented lesions and skin cancer
- ABCDE (Asymmetry, Border, Color variation, Diameter >6 mm, Evolution) plus the **ugly duckling** sign; Evolution matters most. Any changing, bleeding or new lesion in an adult → dermatology within 2 weeks (draft_referral, urgency "soon").
- Rapidly growing nodule (nodular melanoma, Merkel cell) is urgent even if symmetric.
- BCC: pearly papule with telangiectasia, non-healing sore. SCC: keratotic, tender, growing.
- Prevention: SPF 30+, sun avoidance; no routine USPSTF skin screening recommendation for average risk (I statement).

## Common conditions
- **Cellulitis**: unilateral, warm, tender, spreading erythema. Bilateral red legs are usually stasis dermatitis, not cellulitis. Mark borders. Non-purulent: cephalexin; purulent/abscess: drainage ± MRSA coverage (TMP-SMX, doxycycline). Check allergies with draft_prescription.
- **Atopic dermatitis**: emollients, topical steroid matched to site (low potency on face/folds), tacrolimus for face; dupilumab for moderate-severe.
- **Psoriasis**: well-demarcated silvery plaques on extensors/scalp; ask about joint pain (psoriatic arthritis); higher cardiometabolic risk.
- **Acne**: topical retinoid ± benzoyl peroxide first; add oral doxycycline for inflammatory; isotretinoin needs pregnancy prevention (teratogen).
- **Urticaria**: wheals lasting <24 h; second-generation antihistamine (up to 4× dose); lesions lasting >24 h or bruising → urticarial vasculitis workup.
- **Herpes zoster**: dermatomal vesicles; valacyclovir within 72 h; ophthalmic zoster (tip of nose) → same-day eye assessment. Recombinant zoster vaccine at ≥50.
- **Fungal**: KOH/scraping if unsure; never treat a rash with steroid-antifungal combinations long-term.

## Children
Viral exanthems, hand-foot-mouth, impetigo, scabies (treat household), molluscum. Kawasaki (fever ≥5 days + rash + red eyes/lips) and IgA vasculitis (palpable purpura on legs/buttocks: check urine and BP) are must-not-miss.
