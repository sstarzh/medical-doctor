# Pulmonology

## Dyspnea / hypoxemia
- SpO2 target 92–96% (88–92% if hypercapnic risk). A-a gradient differentiates hypoventilation (normal) from V/Q mismatch, shunt, diffusion defect (elevated).
- Rule out PE, ACS/HF, pneumothorax, anaphylaxis early.

## Pulmonary embolism
1. Gestalt + `calc_perc_rule` — if low suspicion and PERC-negative, stop.
2. `calc_wells_pe`: unlikely (≤4) → D-dimer (age-adjusted cut-off: age × 10 µg/L FEU when >50); positive → CTPA. Likely (>4) → CTPA directly.
3. Pregnancy: YEARS-adapted algorithm; V/Q or CTPA acceptable.
4. Treatment: DOAC (apixaban/rivaroxaban) for most; LMWH in cancer is an alternative; massive PE (hypotension) → systemic thrombolysis.

## Asthma (GINA 2024+)
- Avoid SABA-only treatment in adults/adolescents. Preferred track: as-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol (Steps 1–2), maintenance-and-reliever ICS-formoterol (Steps 3–5).
- Severe exacerbation signs: can't speak full sentences, RR >30, HR >120, SpO2 <90%, silent chest → emergency.

## COPD (GOLD 2025)
- Diagnose with post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC <0.70.
- Initial: group A bronchodilator; B → LABA+LAMA; E (exacerbations) → LABA+LAMA, add ICS if eosinophils ≥300.
- Exacerbation: bronchodilators, prednisone 40 mg × 5 days, antibiotics if increased sputum purulence; NIV for hypercapnic acidosis.
- Smoking cessation (varenicline most effective single agent), vaccines, pulmonary rehab.

## Lung nodules
Incidental solid nodule: Fleischner 2017 (by size and risk) — <6 mm low-risk: no routine follow-up; 6–8 mm: CT at 6–12 months; >8 mm: consider PET-CT/biopsy. Screening-detected: Lung-RADS.
Lung cancer screening (USPSTF 2021): age 50–80, ≥20 pack-years, current or quit within 15 years → annual LDCT.

## Obstructive sleep apnea
STOP-BANG screening; home sleep test for uncomplicated adults; CPAP first-line. Consider tirzepatide for OSA with obesity (FDA-approved 2024).
