# Endocrinology

## Type 2 diabetes (ADA Standards of Care, current year)
- Diagnosis: A1c ≥6.5%, fasting glucose ≥126 mg/dL, 2-h OGTT ≥200, or random ≥200 with symptoms (confirm unless unequivocal).
- Individualized A1c goal, typically <7% (less stringent in frail/elderly, hypoglycemia risk).
- Choose agents by comorbidity, independent of A1c:
  - ASCVD or high risk → GLP-1 RA with proven benefit and/or SGLT2i.
  - Heart failure → SGLT2i.
  - CKD (eGFR ≥20, albuminuria) → SGLT2i; add GLP-1 RA (semaglutide, FLOW trial); finerenone if albuminuric.
  - Obesity → GLP-1 RA or dual GIP/GLP-1 RA (tirzepatide).
- Metformin remains reasonable first-line if no compelling indication; reduce dose for eGFR 30–45, stop <30.
- Avoid sulfonylureas/insulin-driven hypoglycemia in older adults.
- Annual: UACR + eGFR, retinal exam, foot exam, lipids; statin for most age 40–75.

## Hyperglycemic crises
- DKA: glucose usually >250 (can be euglycemic on SGLT2i), pH <7.3, HCO3 <18, ketones. Fluids first; IV insulin 0.1 U/kg/h only when K ≥3.5; add dextrose when glucose <250; replace K. Track corrected Na with `calc_corrected_sodium_hyperglycemia` and gap with `calc_anion_gap`.
- HHS: glucose >600, osm >320, minimal ketones; aggressive fluids; slower correction.

## Hypoglycemia
<70 mg/dL level 1, <54 level 2, altered mental status level 3. Rule of 15 (15 g fast carbohydrate, recheck in 15 min). Glucagon for severe. Sulfonylurea hypoglycemia → observe (prolonged); consider octreotide.

## Thyroid
- Hypothyroid: levothyroxine ~1.6 µg/kg/day (start 25–50 µg in elderly/CAD); recheck TSH in 6–8 weeks. Subclinical (TSH <10) often observe.
- Hyperthyroid: identify cause (Graves, toxic nodule, thyroiditis via TRAb/uptake scan); β-blocker for symptoms; methimazole (PTU in first trimester). Thyroid storm → emergency.
- Nodules: ultrasound + TI-RADS determine FNA.

## Adrenal
Adrenal crisis (hypotension, vomiting, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia; on chronic steroids or Addison's) → hydrocortisone 100 mg IV immediately. Sick-day rules for steroid-dependent patients.

## Osteoporosis
Screen women ≥65 (and younger postmenopausal women at risk) with DXA; T-score ≤−2.5 or fragility fracture → treat (bisphosphonate first-line; anabolic agents for very high risk). Check calcium, vitamin D, renal function first.
